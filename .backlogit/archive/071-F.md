Standalone disposable Python preprocessor (scripts/hashicorp_mdx_normalize.py + helper pkg + tests) that selectively copies only the latest-version dir of each versioned HashiCorp product plus all unversioned products from the external read-only corpus, normalizing .mdx->.md in-flight during copy. Produces coverage + unhandled-construct inventory as requirements evidence for a future first-class docline MDX feature. Containment: no session agent writes outside the repo; dry-run default; operator runs --execute. Out of scope: production docline MDX integration.
