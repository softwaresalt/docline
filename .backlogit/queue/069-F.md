Covering feature for the 057-S sitemap residual. Resolves a sitemap hostname exactly once per fetch_sitemap - inside fetch_page where the address is pinned - instead of twice, and reduces validate_sitemap_url to a deterministic resolution-free preflight so its name cannot be mistaken for an authorization decision. fetch_page's authoritative resolve-validate-pin sequence is unchanged.
